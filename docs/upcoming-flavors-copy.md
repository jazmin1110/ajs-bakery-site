## Cookie Butter (WORKING name)

The Biscoff cookie. Keep "Biscoff" out of the name and only use it in the description, because Biscoff is Lotus's trademark.

- **short_description:** Crushed Biscoff in the dough and a creamy Biscoff spread center. Caramel and warm spice.
- **long_description:** Brown butter dough folded with crushed Lotus Biscoff biscuits, wrapped around a creamy Biscoff spread center. You get that caramel, cookie-butter flavor on top of the nutty brown butter, with little bits of biscuit crunch throughout.
- **taste_notes:** ["Caramel and warm spice", "Biscuit crunch", "Creamy center"]

## Peanut Butter Reese's cookie (WORKING name)

- **short_description:** Peanut butter dough with a Reese's piece hidden in the middle. Salty-sweet and soft.
- **long_description:** Brown butter dough with creamy peanut butter mixed in, wrapped around a piece of Reese's. It's salty-sweet peanut butter on top of the nutty brown butter, with a chocolate-peanut middle.
