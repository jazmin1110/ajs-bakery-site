FAQ

When do I get my cookies?

Every Sunday, 3-6pm. Order by Wednesday 9pm for that Sunday; later orders go to the next one.

Pickup or delivery?

Both. You choose at checkout. Delivery is booked by you for the same Sunday window. (Details coming soon.)

How do I pay?

GCash. After checkout you'll see our QR code. Pay, then enter your GCash reference number so we can match it.

What if I don't pay right away?

Unpaid orders are released after 24 hours so someone else can grab the spot.

How many cookies do you bake?

We bake of each flavor per Sunday. Once they're gone, they're gone. If a flavor sells out, the other flavors are still available, and if everything sells out we open the next Sunday.

Do you have nutrition info?

Yes. Every flavor will come with a nutrition label. They're on the way!
